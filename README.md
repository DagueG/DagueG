## About me

AI engineer with nearly 3 years of experience integrating **LLMs and AI agents into real business processes**, each with its own rules, and shipping them to production in demanding environments such as banking and insurance.

Client automation projects are confidential and not published here. This profile shows personal and training projects.

**Product:** I designed and launched [AOfinder](https://aofinder.fr) on my own, an AI-powered SaaS for public procurement monitoring (vector search, LLM re-ranking, automated alerts).
